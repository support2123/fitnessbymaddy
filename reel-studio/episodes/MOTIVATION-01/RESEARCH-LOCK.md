# MOTIVATION-01 — research lock

## Intent

This is a 30-second motivation piece, not a promise of a specific body, treatment, or fast transformation. The motivational spine is **cue → repetition → weekly standard**, rather than shame, miracle results, or a fictional 21-day guarantee.

## Locked evidence used in visible copy

| Visible copy | Evidence boundary | Source chip |
|---|---|---|
| `SET THE CUE.` / `REPEAT THE PROMISE.` | Habit formation is variable; stable context, repeat practice, specific plans, and cues are relevant determinants. It is not guaranteed on a fixed timeline. | `SINGH ET AL. · 2024` |
| `START SMALL. BUILD WEEKLY.` / `150 MINUTES · 2 STRENGTH DAYS` | For general healthy adults, WHO recommends 150–300 minutes of moderate aerobic activity or 75–150 minutes vigorous activity weekly, plus muscle strengthening on 2+ days. People inactive now can begin with small amounts and build. | `WHO · 2020` |

## Safety boundary

The weekly benchmark is a public-health guideline, not a replacement for individual clearance. Start appropriately for your fitness level; seek qualified care for pain, illness, injury, or condition-specific exercise advice.
