# MOTIVATION-LIVE-01 — evidence boundary

This is a music-first motivational reel, not a medical claim or a guaranteed transformation. Its research-aware message is repetition and weekly consistency rather than a made-up fixed habit deadline.

- `CUE. MOVE. REPEAT.`: A 2024 systematic review found habit formation is variable and is influenced by factors including repetition, stable context, specific implementation plans, and cues. It does **not** justify a universal 21-day promise. Singh B, Murphy A, Maher C, Smith AE. *Healthcare* 2024;12(23):2488. https://www.mdpi.com/2227-9032/12/23/2488
- `START SMALL. BUILD WEEKLY. / 150 MINUTES · 2 STRENGTH DAYS`: WHO recommends 150–300 minutes of moderate aerobic activity (or 75–150 vigorous) weekly, and muscle-strengthening activity on 2+ days for general healthy adults. People who are inactive can begin small and build. WHO, 2020. https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/faa83413-d89e-4be9-bb01-b24671aef7ca/content

The reel is general motivation only. Pain, injury, illness, disability, pregnancy, or condition-specific exercise decisions warrant appropriate individual guidance.
