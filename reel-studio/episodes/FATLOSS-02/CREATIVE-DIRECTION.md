# FATLOSS-02 — World-class vertical creative direction

## North-star

**A cinematic scientific documentary compressed into a premium 9:16 hero film.**

The visual language is *Apple Keynote precision × Bloomberg data authority × elite sports broadcast energy*. It must never look like a generic “before/after”, a screaming gym edit, or a template reel.

## Format architecture

- **Master runtime:** ~4:20, designed for the 693-word Clone narration at a natural pace. It is a deep-dive vertical hero film, not a crammed short.
- **Deliverable:** 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264 High, AAC 48 kHz stereo.
- **Cover frame:** the first encoded frame must contain the visual hook and title—never black, never empty.
- **Cutdown plan:** create 60 s, 30 s, and 10 s vertical cutdowns only after the master narrative is approved; never speed up the narration to manufacture a cutdown.

## Chapter board

1. **0:00–0:18 — Kill the slogan**  
   Hero image: a precision balance carrying a restaurant plate on one side and weights on the other. The text `80 / 20?` fractures into `NOT A SCIENTIFIC RATIO.` A live `DEFICIT = INTAKE − EXPENDITURE` equation builds cleanly.

2. **0:18–0:48 — The percentage illusion**  
   A dynamic deficit ledger changes in real time: `−300 FOOD / −100 MOVEMENT = 25% MOVEMENT`, then the percentage dissolves. Line: `THE SPLIT IS MATH. NOT A SLOGAN.`

3. **0:48–1:16 — Which workout wins?**  
   Real, newly licensed human motion only: incline walk, cycling, rowing, swimming and running. Overlay a clean energy-expenditure bar, labelled **illustrative—not a wearable-calorie claim**.

4. **1:16–1:36 — The HIIT correction**  
   Split screen: interval track vs steady cardio; each fills identical energy containers. Big centre verdict: `TIME-EFFICIENT ≠ FAT-LOSS MAGIC.`

5. **1:36–2:08 — The resistance-training reveal**  
   Use a transparent anatomical silhouette with muscle held in cyan and fat mass in gold. The scale de-emphasises; a body-composition dial takes over. `THE SCALE IS NOT THE WHOLE RESULT.`

6. **2:08–2:48 — The hierarchy**  
   Premium protocol cards stack in sequence: FOOD SYSTEM → STRENGTH → AEROBIC BASE → DAILY MOVEMENT → SLEEP. Every card has a single action, one line only.

7. **2:48–3:24 — Invisible levers**  
   Macro food grid, step field, sleep ribbon. Use one controlled sleep claim with a clear small-study qualifier.

8. **3:24–3:57 — Measurement and myths**  
   Seven-day trend chart replaces volatile daily scale values. Water-wave animation wipes one-day readings. Cut the `1,000 CRUNCHES` myth with a red strike—then show `CORE BUILDS MUSCLE. IT DOESN’T CHOOSE FAT.`

9. **3:57–4:20 — Verdict / CTA**  
   Return to the balance hero, now balanced by a complete system. `DIET SETS THE DEFICIT. / CARDIO EXPANDS IT. / LIFTING PROTECTS THE RESULT.` CTA: `COMMENT “SYSTEM” FOR THE 4-WEEK PROTOCOL.`

## Motion rules

- Visual state must change every 1.2–2.5 seconds: camera drift, data update, object relationship, kinetic type, or real-footage cut.
- The cinematic camera moves slowly. Data moves precisely. No random shake, glitch, or zoom-punch.
- Use 180–240 ms spring-like transitions for UI; 600–900 ms cinematic transitions for chapter changes.
- Animate numbers as values, never as arbitrary text. Every bar requires a unit or “illustrative” label.
- Use breathing room after high-density claims. Silence is an editing tool, not dead space.
- No generated human movement. All human exercise clips must be newly sourced, licensed real motion with the licence/source recorded before final export.

## Audio direction

- Start with a single low sub pulse and a restrained tonal texture—no generic motivational drop.
- Music arc: 78 BPM introduction → 122 BPM protocol section → stripped, decisive CTA.
- Voice always wins. Sidechain music under dialogue; transitions use restrained impact, air, and tick SFX.
- Final loudness target: −14 LUFS integrated; true peak at or below −1 dBTP.

## UX acceptance checks

- First frame readable in under one second.
- One idea per screen; a viewer must understand without replaying.
- Every source chip is legible on a phone at arm’s length.
- Text is burnt into the actual export, not dependent on platform captions.
- Captions complement the film; they must not repeat the entire narration line-for-line.
