**You do not get bulky by accident from lifting.**

One session does not rewrite your body. In women-only resistance-training research, training blocks ranged from four weeks to twelve months. And across ten matched-programme studies, women and men showed no significant difference in relative hypertrophy response.

The useful reframe is not “women cannot build muscle.” Women can. The reframe is that muscle change is gradual, trained, and not an accidental side effect of picking up a dumbbell.

For your first week: choose a squat or leg press, a row, and a press. Add a rep before you add weight. Track what gets stronger.

Comment the lift you have been avoiding.

**Sources:** Hagstrom et al., *Sports Medicine*, 2020. Roberts, Nuckols & Krieger, *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research*, 2020.
