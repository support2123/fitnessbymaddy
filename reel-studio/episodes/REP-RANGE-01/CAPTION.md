**The rep range does not decide whether you build muscle. It decides what you practise.**

Light and heavy loads can build similar muscle when sets finish close to failure. Mitchell et al. found thirty percent of max, taken to failure, built as much quadriceps muscle as eighty percent in untrained men over ten weeks. Lopez et al. later found similar hypertrophy across light, moderate, and heavy loading in twenty eight studies and seven hundred forty seven people.

Heavy loads still win for maximal strength. High reps build local muscular endurance. Power needs fast, fresh reps, not grindy ones.

For muscle, use a range you can control and take close to failure. For big lifts, use three to six heavy reps and stop one or two reps short. Aim for ten to twenty hard sets per muscle across the week.

Heavy lifting, learn the movement first. New lifters start moderate.
