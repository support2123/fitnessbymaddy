# REP-RANGE-01 — locked English voice script

**Series:** DECODE  
**Tier:** C teaching reel  
**Target:** 88 seconds; allowed 80–100 seconds; hard cap 110 seconds  
**Voice rule:** Use periods and commas only in spoken copy. Numbers are spelled out. Study names live on-screen only.

## m00 · Hook

Thirty percent of your max built as much muscle as eighty. So why were you told eight to twelve reps.

## m01 · 01 / The Lie

Eight to twelve became the textbook rule. Then it was tested. Light weights and heavy weights, both taken to failure. The muscle grew the same. Twenty eight studies later, the answer held.

## m02 · 02 / The System

But there is a catch. Your body uses its small fibers first. The big fibers, the ones built to grow, switch on as the set gets hard. So a light set only works close to failure. Stop early, or go too light, and the growth falls behind.

## m03 · 03 / The Trade

So what do reps decide. Not how much muscle. What it is good at. One to six heavy reps build the most strength. Fifteen to thirty build endurance. Power is fast, few and fresh. End the set when the bar slows. And bones and tendons respond best to heavy load.

## m04 · 04 / The Receipt

Same program, five hundred eighty five people. Muscle growth ranged from nothing to almost sixty percent. Your genes set the ceiling. Effort decides how close you get.

## m05 · 05 / The Protocol

The protocol. Big lifts, three to six heavy reps, a rep or two short of failure. For muscle, anywhere from six to thirty reps, finished close to failure. Ten to twenty hard sets per muscle, every week.

## m06 · CTA

Stop counting reps. Count how close you get. Comment thirty if nobody told you this. Next, how many sets a week it really takes.

## Locked accuracy language

- Say **as much as** and **similar**, never identical or proven the same.
- Eight to twelve is efficient, not wrong.
- Heavy compound sets stop one to two reps short; failure is not a requirement.
- High reps refer to local muscular endurance, not cardio fitness.
- The five hundred eighty five-person spread is an individual-variation bound, not a promise.
