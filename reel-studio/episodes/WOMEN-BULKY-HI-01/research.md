# RESEARCH — WOMEN-BULKY-01

**Topic:** Women do not get bulky from lifting
**Tier:** B, 30–60 seconds, myth + fix
**Locked category:** WOMEN'S PHYSIOLOGY
**Core question:** Does resistance training accidentally make women bulky?

## Research verdict

The reel must not say women cannot build muscle. They can, and that is a useful adaptation. The accurate reframe is narrower: **lifting does not accidentally make a woman bulky after a few sessions.** Muscle adaptation is deliberate and gradual, not an automatic one-dumbbell outcome.

## Citation spine

| Use | Locked claim | On-screen citation | Confidence / bound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slow adaptation | Female-only resistance-training studies ran **4 weeks to 12 months** and produced strength gains plus a moderate hypertrophy effect. | **HAGSTROM ET AL. · SPORTS MED · 2020** | T1 meta-analysis. Do not predict an individual outcome. |
| Relative response | Across 10 studies using the same programme, men and women had **no significant difference in relative hypertrophy response**. | **ROBERTS ET AL. · J STRENGTH COND RES · 2020** | T1 meta-analysis. This means women can build muscle, not that every person changes identically. |
| Myth context | Women carry around **10–15× less testosterone** and hypertrophy is slow and effortful. | **ACSM · ENDOCRINE REFERENCE RANGES** | T2. This is background only; it is not the reel's hero statistic because an author/year source is stronger for the visual proof. |
