Original 96 BPM micro-techno / athletic score: no vocal sample, no melody mimic. Narration has priority. Duck bed by 6–8 dB over every spoken phrase; reserve transitions for sub impacts, filtered percussion, sample-free ticks, and one end-card resolve.
