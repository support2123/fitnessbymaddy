**Most supplements solve a marketing problem. Start with the gap.**

Protein powder is convenience when food cannot cover your protein needs—not a compulsory health product. B12, vitamin D, and iron are decisions for an actual dietary, life-stage, medication, or lab-based reason. Creatine monohydrate is a well-supported option for resistance training, but it is optional too.

The red flags: fat burners, detoxes, testosterone boosters, and mystery blends. If you are pregnant, use medication, or have kidney or liver disease, check with your clinician or pharmacist before adding supplements.
