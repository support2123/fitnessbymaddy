## m00 · HOOK · 0.3–4.2 s

Eighty percent diet, twenty percent exercise. No study ever measured that.

## m01 · 01 / THE PHYSICS · 4.6–17.1 s

Where does fat go? It does not melt, or turn into muscle. Lose ten kilos of fat, and eight point four leave through your lungs, as carbon dioxide. The rest, as water. One law. Burn more than you take in, long enough.

## m02 · 02 / THE SPLIT · 17.5–37.7 s

Cardio alone, at typical doses, takes off about one and a half kilos in six months. Why so little? Your body fights back. On average, it cancels over a quarter of what exercise burns. But add training to the same diet, and in one review, people lost three kilos more. A year later, diet plus training was still ahead.

## m03 · 03 / THE VERDICT · 38.6–45.2 s

So, not eighty twenty. Diet drives the loss. Training decides what you lose, and whether it stays off.

## m04 · 04 / THE WORKOUT · 45.5–61.7 s

Which workout cuts the most fat? Minute for minute, cardio. Intervals or steady? About the same, just faster. Lifting protects muscle. In older dieters, cardio alone cost five percent of lean mass. With lifting, two or three. The winner is both.

## m05 · 05 / THE HIDDEN LEVERS · 62.0–82.1 s

The levers people miss. Exercise shrinks the fat around your organs, even when the scale barely moves. In one small lab study, dieters sleeping five and a half hours, instead of eight and a half, lost less than half the fat. On high protein, while dieting and training hard, young men lost nearly five kilos of fat in four weeks, and gained lean mass.

## m06 · 06 / THREE MYTHS · 82.5–89.1 s

Three myths, gone. Crunches do not shrink belly fat. Afterburn is small. Fasted cardio gives no extra fat loss.

## m07 · 07 / THE PROTOCOL · 89.5–107.2 s

The protocol. Lose half to one percent of your weight weekly. Protein, at least one point six grams per kilo, plus fibre. Lift two or three times a week. Cardio, two and a half to five hours a week. Sleep at least seven hours. Track the trend, and adjust slowly. People who keep it off move about an hour a day.

## m08 · CTA · 107.6–114.5 s

Diet moves the scale. Training decides what comes off. Comment eighty if you believed eighty twenty. Next, why the scale stalls.
