## Accuracy locks

- Never claim HIIT is superior to steady cardio for fat loss.
- Never claim spot reduction, fat turning into muscle, sweat being fat, or all-day afterburn.
- Never call the living 10 kg clock measured data; it is an explicitly illustrative episode signature.
- Do not generalise sleep, protein, or lean-mass results beyond their displayed study population.
- Always keep the m07 safety line visible ≥3 seconds: `PREGNANT, INJURED, MANAGING DIABETES OR MEDICATION, OR AN EATING-DISORDER HISTORY? GET PERSONAL GUIDANCE FROM A QUALIFIED CLINICIAN.`
