# FAT-LOSS-SYSTEM-01 — world-class visual direction

## Format and pacing

- **Delivery target:** 1080 × 1920, 30 fps; ~100 seconds; no black opening; cover equals frame zero.
- **Shot language:** 16–20 new, license-cleared real moving fitness shots—walking/incline, cycling, rower, intervals, barbell, dumbbells, meal preparation, plate/portion context, daily movement, measurement/check-in. Never reuse background video from an earlier reel.
- **Editing rhythm:** hard visual beat every 2–3 seconds; no still hold beyond 1.5 seconds; camera motion even within b-roll; three intentional half-second silence/visual-reset beats before the key corrections.
- **Sound:** original 150–160 BPM “precision-hybrid” score. Beat muted by 7–9 dB below narration; rises between statements. Drop at `SO WHICH WORKOUT?`; low-sub impact at `HIIT IS NOT MAGIC`; warm final resolve under brand.

## Design system

- **Palette:** graphite `#071014`, bone `#F3F1EA`, evidence cyan `#44DCE4`, metabolic lime `#C9F05B`, correction red `#FF5B4D`, restrained gold `#E3B34F`.
- **Typography:** condensed bold grotesk for headlines; technical mono for data/source chips. One statement at a time; maximum two lines; 70–86 px headline equivalent on 1080 canvas; 24–28 px utility copy.
- **UI:** premium “Energy Ledger” rail: `INTAKE` above / `EXPENDITURE` below / `DEFICIT` locks only after the script says repeatable calorie deficit. It must visually update chapter-by-chapter, not remain static.
- **Research chips:** source chip stays on screen for every numerical/statistical claim:
  - `JAMA NETW OPEN · 2024 · 116 RCTs` for 150/300 min and average values
  - `HIIT vs MICT · SR/MA · 2023` for the non-magic correction
  - `RESISTANCE + DIET · SR/MA · 2025` for lean-mass/fat-mass point
  - `WHO · 2020` for 150 min + 2 strength days baseline

## Chapter map

| Time | Spoken idea | Actual video background | Kinetic visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–6 s | No magic workout / hierarchy | Athlete pauses before treadmill / training floor | `FAT LOSS HAS NO MAGIC WORKOUT.` slams in; hierarchy ladder assembles |
| 6–19 s | Deficit / no 80–20 split | food prep → smart grocery → incline walk | Fake `80 / 20` gauge fractures; `NO UNIVERSAL PERCENTAGE.` locks over Energy Ledger |
| 19–39 s | Aerobic dose-response | incline walk, bike, rower, outdoor brisk walk | 150→300 min circular progress engine; JAMA chip; stat card appears only after the voice names the number |
| 39–52 s | HIIT correction | sprint intervals contrasted with steady incline/cardio | Red `MAGIC` stamp rejected; two equal “work” meters converge |
| 52–65 s | Resistance role | squat, row, press, controlled dumbbell work | Scale icon de-emphasised; lean-mass shield and strength signal animate |
| 65–82 s | Winning stack | meal plate, barbell, walking calls, recovery/sleep room | Five-card stack: `DEFICIT / PROTEIN+FIBRE / LIFT / CARDIO / DAILY MOVEMENT` |
| 82–92 s | tracking / safety | weekly check-in → clinician or coach conversation | noisy daily scale line resolves into a downward weekly trend; lower-third: `GENERAL EDUCATION · PERSONALISE FOR MEDICAL CONTEXT` |
| 92–100 s | brand resolve | athlete walking into gym | final brand card: `FITNESSBYMADDY / FAT LOSS IS A SYSTEM.` |

## UX guards

- Do not decorate every frame with every chart. One dominant visual idea at a time.
- All visible data gets its source chip; source chip must sit above common app UI zones.
- Do not show before/after bodies, calorie-burn numbers, miracle promises, or shame-based copy.
- Captions on during education; off for the final two-second FitnessByMaddy payoff.
