# OPENING-IMAGE + FIRST-FRAME / COVER DOCTRINE
### FitnessByMaddy science reels — the law for frame 0

Every reel is born on a full-bleed hero image. There is no "title card." There is no black open. Frame 0 is a poster that happens to move.

---

## 1 · THE PRINCIPLE — the first frame IS the poster

Frame 0 does three jobs at once, or it fails:

- **Stops the scroll** — competes with 100 other reels in a 0.4s thumb-flick. It must be a *visual event*, not a label.
- **Survives as the cover** — Instagram freezes and displays this frame in the feed, the profile grid, Explore, and shares. It has to be gorgeous at 1080px AND legible at ~150px.
- **Foreshadows + interrogates** — it plants the topic and opens a question ("what is that?", "is that inside me?", "wait, what happens next?") without spoiling the payoff.

**Hard law:** The topic/title is *integrated into* a full-bleed image — sitting in reserved negative space of a living scene — never floated on flat black, never a "WHAT WE COVER" bullet card. If a designer could screenshot the open and it reads as a slideshow slide, it is rejected.

**Text-on-image rule:** The image model generates the scene **with no baked-in text**. Typography is composited in the edit (Bebas Neue / Anton / Druk Wide) over the reserved zone. This gives font control, keeps text crisp at thumbnail, and enforces the brand guardrail: **the word "AI" never appears in on-screen text**, ever.

## 2 · TAXONOMY — 12 psychological hero-image concepts

Each = named concept · generation-ready prompt (feed to image model, then upscale + img2video) · the emotion it fires · best topic fit. All prompts assume **9:16, no text baked in, top or upper-mid negative space reserved**.

**Generation stack (Lifetime Rule):** Midjourney V8 for surreal/stylized concepts (uncanny scale, elemental, haunting object); Gemini Imagen / photoreal engine for skin, faces, anatomy realism; Flux + LoRA for Maddy likeness consistency; Magnific/Topaz upscale to crisp 1080×1920; then Runway Gen-4 / Kling / Veo turn the still into the 1–2s living hero.

**3. THE SINGLE HAUNTING OBJECT** — one isolated object, hyperreal, wrong.

## 3 · COMPOSITION RULES for 9:16 (1080 × 1920)

**The safe-zone map (memorize):**
- **Bottom ~330px (y 1590–1920):** DEAD. Username, caption, audio, CTA live here. No focal element, no title.
- **Right rail ~120px (x 960–1080):** DEAD. Like/comment/share/more icons. Keep the subject's key mass and any title off this strip.
- **Top corners (0–250px):** partially obscured (sound tag, top chrome). Keep clean.
- **Live hero zone:** x 60–960, y 250–1560. Everything that must read lives here.

**Focal-point placement:** Put the hero subject's center of gravity at **~x540, y820** — upper-center, just above the vertical midline. This keeps it in the always-visible zone AND centers it for the square/4:5 crop the profile grid uses.

**Negative space for the title:** Reserve ONE clean, low-detail band — either **top (y 280–560)** or **upper-mid (y 1120–1480, still above the UI)**. Design the image so this band is dark, soft, or empty so composited type reads without a scrim. Never let the subject fight the title.

**Contrast + subject scale:** One dominant shape occupying **40–65% of frame height**, carved out of shadow by rim/edge light. If you squint and it's not instantly one readable silhouette, it's too busy.

**The thumbnail test (non-negotiable):** Downscale to 150px and center-crop to 1:1. If the concept doesn't read — if you can't tell what it is or where to look — reject it. Rules that pass the test: single subject, extreme figure/ground contrast, one light source, no fine text, no clutter.

**Structure:** one subject, one light, one question. Max two accent colors. Deep falloff so the eye is *forced* to the focal detail.

## 4 · THE COVER-FRAME LOCK + living reveal

**Guaranteeing the hero IS the cover (belt + suspenders):**
1. **Hold frame 0 static for 15–30 frames (0.5–1.0s)** before any motion begins, so scrubbers, auto-thumbnails, and previews all land on the hero.
2. **Export the exact hero as a matching still PNG (1080×1920)** and, in the IG upload flow, **manually set it as the cover** ("Cover → From camera roll / Add from gallery"). Never trust IG's auto-pick — it grabs a mid-motion blur.
3. The uploaded cover still and frame 0 must be **pixel-identical** so there's zero jump when the reel starts playing (compression can shift frame 0; the uploaded still overrides that).
4. Compose the cover still with the **type already burned in** (the version people see in-grid), while the reel's frame 0 can hold the type pre-animation — keep them visually matched.

**The living reveal — motion comes FROM the hero, never a cut to black:**
The first 1–2s must make the poster *breathe*, then reveal the topic ON the living image:

- **Slow push-in:** scale 1.00 → 1.06 over 1.2s, ease-out. Adds life without leaving the frame.
- **Parallax:** if layered (subject / mid haze / background), drift layers at different speeds for depth.
- **Focus-pull (rack focus):** open soft (subject slightly out of focus) → snap sharp on the focal detail at ~0.6s. Feels like the reel "wakes up."
- **Atmosphere drift:** dust, embers, mist, or particles moving slowly across the frame — the single biggest "alive vs. static" signal.
- **Accent-glow pulse:** the gold light source breathes (bloom/halation swells ~8%) once, timed to the first beat/impact.
- **Title reveal ON the image:** type animates in over the held hero via mask-wipe, blur-in, or track-in — never a hard cut to a separate card. The topic *emerges from* the scene.

**Tooling:** generate still → upscale (Magnific/Topaz) → img2video in Runway Gen-4 / Kling / Veo for the push/particle/parallax → assemble + type + grade in DaVinci Resolve (or Remotion for programmatic). Sidechain nothing yet; the open is visual.

## 5 · COLOR / LIGHTING / MOOD — "premium + mind-bending"

**Palette (locked):**
- Base / background: near-black **#0A0B0E** → graphite **#101115**
- Primary accent glow: brand gold **#D4A148**, highlight **#E8BC6A**
- Neutral/subject: bone **#F5F1E8** for skin/highlight lift
- Optional cold counter-accent (science/threat only, sparingly): clinical cyan **~#1FB6C9** as a rim, never dominant

**Lighting doctrine — "one light, deep dark":**
- **ONE hero light source.** Motivated, directional, warm gold. Everything else falls into shadow.
- **~80% of the frame in shadow.** Subject carved out by rim/edge light. Negative fill on the shadow side.
- **High micro-contrast on the focal detail**, deep falloff everywhere else — the darkness does the framing.
- Volumetric haze / light shafts to give the single beam a body.

**Grade spec (Editorial Athletic house grade):**
- Skin luminance 62–70 IRE; gold highlights ride just under clip (~95 IRE), never blown.
- Shadows crushed but **not** pure black — leave detail floor at ~5 IRE (premium, not muddy).
- Gentle **halation/bloom** on the gold glow; **vignette −0.10 to −0.15**.
- Fine film grain **0.3 intensity on hero stills only** (keep motion clean).
- Reference real LUTs (CinemaCC / Lutify Clean Athletic ~40% mix) — no default filters.

**Mood in one line:** a dark museum with a single gold spotlight on something your body has been hiding from you.
