# DOCTRINE-04 · LIVE MOTION BACKGROUND
## FBM reel doctrine — every beat is a moving film, never a held poster

**Status:** locked operating doctrine · English-Latin on screen · secular · Maddy = he/him · no visible AI label · 9:16 / 1080×1920 / 30 fps.

This supersedes every legacy behaviour in which one generated still is parked behind a reel and text changes over it. It upgrades the pipeline; it never lowers the prior research, hero-open, clock, type, audio, or publish bars.

---

## 0 · WHY THIS EXISTS

The Women-Bulky audit confirmed that hero opens, Tier-B pacing, cited proof, honest reframes, −14 LUFS, and scene variety had improved. It also confirmed the remaining primary defect: per-beat images were frozen. Consecutive frames half a second apart were identical; a press frame did not contain a press, and a squat frame did not contain a squat.

A motion-poster is not a film. The image must carry narrative motion while type remains legible.

---

## 1 · THE NON-NEGOTIABLE LAW

1. **No held static photo anywhere in the story.** A background may never be a frozen or near-frozen image for more than **2.0 seconds**.
2. **Every story beat owns a different moving shot.** Hook/fear, mechanism, proof/graph, protocol, and CTA/resolve each receive their own clip.
3. **A beat longer than its clip loops or extends with live motion.** Never freeze the final frame to fill time.
4. **Text, citation, and clock composite over the moving clip.** They never replace movement with a dead template panel.
5. **Motion-poster = FAIL. Living film = SHIP.**

Each beat has exactly one dominant moving subject. Dust, particles, grain-free atmosphere, and other secondary movement remain at 20–40% visual strength. Background velocity must never fight spoken words.

---

## 2 · HOW LIVE CLIPS ARE PRODUCED

### 2A · Generation lanes

| Need | Preferred lane | Required handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cinematic still animation / reference-led camera | Runway Gen-4 / Gen-4 Turbo | 1080p, 5–10s, 24/30fps, seed locked; Gen-4 references for subject continuity; Aleph only for legitimate video-to-video revision. |
| Human physical realism: lifts, breath, physics | Kling 2.5 | Start/end frame or motion brush; isolate the working body region; professional mode; 5–10s. |
| Photoreal atmosphere and resolve | Veo 3.1 | 1080p or higher source; 8s cinematic camera; mute native audio; use for fear/atmosphere/hero resolve. |
| Longer environment or abstract continuity | Sora 2 | 15–20s storyboard only when the beat requires it; avoid as a likeness shortcut. |
| Keyframe mechanism reveal | Pika 2.2 | 3–5s keyframe transition for skin→muscle→fascia or related reveal. |
| Seamless ambient loop | Luma Ray 3 | Native loop mode, 5s, controlled camera drift. |
| Style lock | Midjourney V7 video | Lock style reference and animate the approved still rather than rerolling the character. |

**Continuity law:** lock one seed, one style reference, and one approved reference image for every generated human shot in a reel. Do not freely reroll a character per beat.

### 2B · Real footage lanes

Use authentic footage whenever human hands, loaded anatomy, sweat, breath, or gym texture matter more than synthetic control.

- **Artgrid:** premium cinematic gym, body, lab and graded-friendly plates.
- **Storyblocks:** motion texture, utility b-roll, particle and environmental plates.
- **Pexels / Mixkit:** license-reviewed fast fills, dust/particle plates, atmosphere.
- **Envato Elements / Motion Array:** overlays, particles, light, LUT-ready plates.

Prefer real footage for actual lifting, grip, breath, sweat, treadmill/track, food prep, and MRI/lab environments.

### 2C · Anatomy and abstract motion

- Complete Anatomy / BioDigital capture for organ pulse, diaphragm rise/fall, muscle contraction, fascia glide, or cross-section reveal.
- Blender / custom 3D for rigged anatomy camera work, fluids, particles, and metabolic abstracts.
- Every anatomical system must animate. A static organ cross-section violates this doctrine.

### 2D · Prompt grammar

**Ambient / fear / CTA**

```text
[subject], extreme slow cinematic push-in, near-static handheld micro-drift,
volumetric dust drifting slowly right-to-left, shallow depth of field,
graphite low-key lighting, single soft key from upper-left, 5s seamless loop,
no cuts, no fast motion, subject stays centered
```

**Protocol / action**

```text
[athlete] performing [movement] with controlled tempo, sweat visible,
camera slow dolly-in on the working muscle, real physics, natural weight,
5s, motion isolated to the body, background steady, cinematic contrast
```

**Anatomy**

```text
cross-section of [organ/muscle], slow rhythmic pulse or contraction cycle,
subsurface glow, medical-cinematic, camera slow orbit 15 degrees, loopable
```

All generation prompts specify: 9:16, 1080×1920, 30fps master, muted output, upper-left key, 4800–5200K, no embedded captions/logos/UI/watermarks, and at least one second of trim headroom.

### 2E · Timing, loops, and delivery media

- Generate ambient clips at 5s and action clips at 8–10s; generate at least one second longer than required.
- Loop preference: native loop → matching start/end frame → 12–15 frame editorial crossfade. Ping-pong is allowed only for abstract particles, never human movement or camera pushes.
- Generate at the best native resolution, upscale before grade where needed, and master all clips at **30fps**. Interpolate where needed; never duplicate frames.
- Deliver only a muted vertical `.mp4` per selected beat. A `.jpg` is never a selected background asset.

## 3 · MOTION GRAMMAR

| Beat | Background behaviour | Speed law |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook / fear | slow ominous push, drifting dust, restrained micro-instability | camera ≤6% frame travel over beat |
| Mechanism | organ pulse, breath, contraction, fascia glide, particle flow | rhythmic; orbit ≤15° |
| Proof / graph | a line or fixed data mark draws over a living graphite/cyan ground | draw in 0.6–1.2s; ground drift ≤4% |
| Protocol | real athlete or credible generated physical execution | natural tempo; dolly ≤8% |
| CTA / resolve | confident forward hero push and controlled gold resolve | push 5–8%; no shake |

Text/citation/clock animate in over the first 6–12 frames of the beat. Motion moves in one slow direction; no whips, speed ramps, or cut clutter within a beat.

---

## 4 · ONE-FILM CONTINUITY

Different sources must read as one film:

- One **Editorial Athletic** grade over every selected clip.
- Palette: graphite `#101115`, gold `#D4A148` / `#E8BC6A`, cyan `#3FC6E0`, red `#FF1F1F`, bone `#F5F1E8`.
- White-balance-match every clip to **4800–5200K** before the house grade.
- Key light remains upper-left in generated prompts; reject a shot that reverses it without a story reason.
- Camera language is slow pushes/dollies. No arbitrary whip pan or orbit unless it is a deliberate reel-wide rule.
- **Grain = 0.** Blacks, contrast, saturation, and vignette are matched across all clips; no per-clip drift.

## 5 · PIPELINE CONTRACT

### 5A · Required stage

```text
Script → Voice → Timeline → Hero Open
      → ASSET / B-ROLL (new: per-beat live clips + manifest)
      → Score → Film (kinetic type + prominent clock over clips)
      → Motion QC + technical QC → Fresh Eyes → Publish
```

### 5B · Manifest

Every episode writes `motion-manifest.json` and `MOTION-GENERATION-PACK.md` through `tools/engine/motion_manifest.py`.

Every row requires:

- `id`, measured `start_s` and `end_s`
- `clip_file` ending in `.mp4`
- source/provider lane, `motion_type`, loop decision, and one hero subject
- grade note and color-match requirement
- prompt, seed, reference image, style reference, source duration, and cache key

No row can pass strict validation with a still image path or a missing file. Raw, graded, and reroll versions are append-only; the manifest alone selects the current version.

### 5C · Composite order

1. live beat clip
2. source color match
3. Editorial Athletic house grade
4. vignette / optional restrained atmosphere plate
5. kinetic type
6. lifted citations
7. prominent Living Clock
8. safe-zone inspection layer during review

### 5D · Caching and versioning

Cache identity is the hash of prompt + seed + source/reference + settings. Reuse a selected clip between re-renders. Version rerolls `_v2`, `_v3`; never overwrite an approved source. Keep raw and graded clip outputs distinct.

### 5E · Automatic hard gate

`tools/engine/motion_qc.py` evaluates approved beat clips **before delivery**:

1. Farnebäck optical-flow energy rejects any static/near-static two-second window.
2. Mid-frame perceptual hash rejects reused shots masquerading as different beats.
3. Freeze-tail inspection rejects last-frame padding.
4. Luma/chroma continuity warns on grade drift.
5. Manifest validation enforces 1080×1920 / 30fps contract, `.mp4` assets, full timeline coverage, visible-motion policy, and continuity data.

The first three are delivery blockers. A failure returns to ASSET / B-ROLL.

---

## 6 · THE ONLY STILL FALLBACK

When a real or generated clip is truly impossible, the fallback must contain **all four**:

1. at least three segmented depth planes (foreground / midground / background), with inpainted background where required;
2. depth-map displacement or camera projection (Depth Anything / MiDaS / Fusion / AE equivalent);
3. slow drifting particle or atmosphere plate at 20–40%; and
4. a continuous one-direction 3–6% camera push.

A flat scaled JPEG, a regular Ken-Burns move, or particles over an unseparated still is still static and fails.
