## Feed caption

**Water is the only thing you run out of faster than motivation.**

Every single day your heart pushes about 7,500 litres of fluid through you. You hold five.
Lose one percent of your body weight as water and your heart rate climbs roughly three beats a
minute at the same pace. Same work. More beats.

The 8-glasses rule? That is one line from a 1945 report that everyone read halfway.
And past thirst, in long events, plain water can outrun your sodium. At least 14 athletes have
died of it since 1981.

So here is the protocol, and it costs you one weigh-in. Weigh before and after a hard session.
Every kilo lost is a litre. Replace 1.2 to 1.5 times that over the next three hours. Stay below
two percent, which is about 1.4 kilos at 70.

Comment **WATER** and tell me how much you sweated out today. I read every one.

Save this for your next long session, and send it to the friend who only drinks water.
