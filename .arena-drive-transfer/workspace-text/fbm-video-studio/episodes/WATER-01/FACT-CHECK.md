## 3 · Kahan humne jaan-boojh kar "soft" language rakhi (honest bounding)

- **"≈ / about" wale numbers:** 7,500 L, 180 L, 42 L, 1.4 kg — ye textbook estimates aur simple
  arithmetic hain, koi exact measurement nahi. Screen aur VO dono me "about / lagbhag" hai.
- **+3 bpm** ek **average association** hai systematic review se, personal law nahi — individual
  variation hota hai. Isliye screen pe likha hai "CLAIMED FOR EVERY 1%..." (claimed, not proven-for-you).
- **EAH "14 deaths" = floor, ceiling nahi.** Surveillance adhoora hai; consensus "at least 14" kehta hai.
  Aur ye deaths **zyada paani** se hui, paani kam hone se nahi — risk factor: 4 ghante se lamba event
  + aasani se uplabdh fluids.
- **Cramps:** hum "not proven" kehte hain, "salt ka koi role nahi" nahi. Evidence mixed hai; isliye
  video me "sodium is a maybe" line hai.
- **8×8:** claim "healthy adults ke liye basis nahi" hai. **Exceptions jo humne video me nahi bole**
  (kidney stones, garmi/namii, mushkil physical kaam) — agar koi comment me pooche to jawab yahi rahega
  ki exceptions exist karti hain; ye hamari known omission hai, isliye pinned reply me aa sakti hai.
- **Brain 73% (T3):** purana widely-cited source hai. Screen pe cite chip Buono/Gray's ka hai —
  technically Mitchell 1945 ko naam karna chahiye tha. Chhota citation-precision nit (§5 me listed).
