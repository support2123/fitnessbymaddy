WHY FILES KEPT VANISHING (found 24 Sep 2026)
  The workspace snapshot is best-effort capped near 128 MB. With two 1080x1920 masters
  (95 MB together) plus assets, this repo already sits at ~121 MB — so any large new
  artifact pushed it over, the turn-end snapshot silently failed, and the NEXT restore
  reverted to an older state. That is why the freshly built viewer copies disappeared
  twice while the masters survived. Rules that follow from it:
    · keep total workspace <= ~120 MB, prune before adding
    · viewers must stay tiny: tools/engine/make_viewer.py builds both in ~90 s, 4.3 MB total
    · the in-app preview uses WATER-01-PLAYER.html (everything embedded, cannot 404)
