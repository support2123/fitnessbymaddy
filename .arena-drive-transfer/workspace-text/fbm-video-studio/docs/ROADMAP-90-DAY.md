**Exit criteria:** presets in place · 30+ locked claims · bio live · pinned trio published · nothing named after an element in public view.

## THE 10-ITEM BRAND-SAFETY CHECKLIST (run at every publish)
1. Category words present (decode / sourced / Body-IDs), sector words absent (fitness / transformation / secrets).
2. Citation visible on screen **and** in caption — author + year + journal.
3. Numbers wear units and match the Number Registry (one value, everywhere).
4. Secular-not-woo: zero Forbidden Lexicon; no elemental labels on surface.
5. "Mind" is science, not spirit — cortisol/HRV/sleep science, cite-or-delete.
6. HE/HIM 100 %; NASM-CPT lower-third on talking-head; peer tier leads with the citation.
7. Confidence tier visible where it matters ("emerging evidence" / "mechanistically").
8. Attack claims, not people.
9. No fear-bait, no bait-and-switch, no fake scarcity; 85/15 respected; CTA passes the sell-out test.
10. End on a decision (Scan/plan) — never "like and follow".
**Plus the Contradiction Gate** (Critic 3.4): "does this contradict a prior post?" — if yes, reconcile in-frame before publishing.
